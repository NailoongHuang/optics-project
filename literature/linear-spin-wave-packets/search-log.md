## Screening and deduplication

- Deduplicate by arXiv base identifier. A version update is not another paper.
- Main group: 2108.08054, 2111.02236, 1906.02301, 1703.05548, 2301.01220, 2404.15011.
- Transferable optical-method group: 2109.00782, 2201.08297, 1711.04027.
- Distinguish direct pulse-width measurements from group-velocity evidence, monochromatic transverse collimation, and steady-state structured beams.
- Do not classify every full-LLG simulation as a strictly linear solver. Record explicit linear assumptions or weak excitation where stated, and disclose absent amplitude-scaling verification.
- Exclude stationary flat-band localization as evidence of moving packets, nonlinear solitons as the requested mechanism, and optical/electronic spin textures as direct magnetic spin-wave implementations.
- Keep the 2010 Schneider and 2021 Dai publisher records as supplemental leads without claiming a verified arXiv copy or downloaded full text.

## Stopping condition and limitations

Stopped after the main pulse-preservation experiment, a geometry-control follow-up, Bessel-like beam shaping, collimation, caustic classification, recent caustic beam generation, and optical spectral-correlation methods had been identified and their relevant full text checked. Additional broad variants increasingly returned unrelated optical spin, atomic memory, flat bands, and nonlinear propagation.

This is a bounded topic search, not a systematic all-records review or a novelty certificate. Failure to find a magnetic space-time-wave-packet implementation is not proof that none exists. Search-engine discovery coverage can omit papers, and the absence of a verified arXiv version does not imply the absence of a publisher or institutional copy.
