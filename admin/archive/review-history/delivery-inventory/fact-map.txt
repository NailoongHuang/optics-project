1. Suggested main line

这项工作从一个简单问题出发：在一排排相邻自旋之间，一个局部激发怎样向外传播？
先把模型和色散关系理顺，再把传播过程画出来；随后比较哪些初始波包散得更慢，
解释动画里的明暗变化，并检查文献中哪些结果已经有人做过。
最后从论文留下的具体问题里选出四个方向，各做了一次小规模、带对照的试算。
现在已经有了推导、可运行代码、数据、动画和文献，可以据此选择下一步，而不只停留在一张好看的图上。

Suggested reader-facing conclusion:
最值得继续的是让更窄的波束真正给接收器带来更多信号；
反流的检测也值得研究，但测量要求较高。
损耗补偿要看任务是否需要保留相位；无序比较目前更适合作为校准基准。

2. Work stages and the few facts that matter

A. Model and derivation
Plain explanation:
模型把每个位置上的自旋与相邻位置连接起来。只翻转一个自旋时，
激发可以在这些位置之间移动；色散关系告诉我们不同波长的成分怎样积累相位。
后续模拟直接从这条色散关系出发。
Current source:
  Note/single_magnon_derivation/main.tex
  Note/single_magnon_derivation/main.pdf
  Note/single_magnon_derivation/review.md
The accepted PDF is 2 pages; the current review record is dated 2026-10-20.
The report need not reproduce the Coulomb-to-spin algebra; one final dispersion formula is enough.

B. Simulation
Plain explanation:
先给每个格点一个初始振幅，再把它分解成不同波长的模式。
每个模式按自己的能量演化，最后叠加回格点。这给出每个时刻的概率分布。
Current one-magnon figures use exact Fourier evolution, not the early real-spin RK4 animation.
Useful source:
  figures/midterm-report/common.py
  figures/midterm-report/THEORY.md
  figures/midterm-report/notation/README.md
The early general solver remains useful:
  code/spin_wave_2d.py
  code/test_spin_wave_2d.py
but it should not be used to describe the method of the later one-magnon movies.

C. Why choose the two-direction crossing
Plain explanation:
波包由一小段波长组成。如果这些成分跑得不一样快，波包就会拉开。
只让一个方向的二阶色散消失，只能减弱那个方向的展宽；
两个方向同时满足条件，波包就能更久地保持紧凑。
Minimal numbers:
  Same initial RMS width 6a, Jy=Jx, t=180 hbar/Jx:
  A ordinary point: widths (12.924a,12.924a).
  B one-direction point: (6.064a,12.924a).
  C crossing: (6.064a,6.064a).
Later:
  At t=4320 hbar/Jx, C widths are about 21.975a and the shifted-state overlap is 0.404.
  The crossing delays deformation; it does not stop it permanently.
Optional lifetime numbers, only if needed:
  F first reaches 0.9 at A/B/C = 62.9/91.2/1127.1 hbar/Jx for initial width 6a.
  Initial-width scaling across 4a,6a,8a,12a is about sigma^2 for A/B and sigma^3 for C.
Source:
  figures/midterm-report/wavepacket-audit/tracking/results.json

D. The red-blue flicker
Plain explanation:
红蓝图显示的是复振幅的一部分。相位在转动时，颜色会变浅、变深甚至换色，
但概率包络不一定同时改变。把实部和概率放在一起看，就能分清这两种变化。
Minimal numbers:
  t=9 hbar/Jx: real absolute peak / initial = 0.7464,
  probability peak / initial = 0.99982.
The movie’s sparse time samples make the phase variation look slower.
Avoid adding the entire checkerboard/cos-sin derivation to the report.
Source:
  figures/midterm-report/crossing-diagnostics/dynamics/results.json
  figures/midterm-report/crossing-diagnostics/phase_probability_comparison.png

E. What literature changed
Plain explanation:
查到的论文表明，选择 ka=pi/2 附近的波包来减弱展宽，是已有的方法。
早期自旋链研究已经讨论了剩余的三阶色散，二维光学阵列也做过两个方向的比较。
因此，下一步应围绕真实的制备、损耗和接收问题提出新任务，而不是把“曲率为零”重新命名。
Direct sources:
  Osborne & Linden 2004; Chen et al. 2007; Longhi 2009; Thompson et al. 2016.
Recent value:
  Meier et al. 2025 uses related low-spreading transport in a quantum-clock task.
Keep the literature comparison short in the body. Full titles/versions belong in the paper index.

F. Four minimum tests

F1. Finite-source directional beam
Reader wording:
更窄、更亮的波束，只有落进接收器才有用。
试算中近高阶点在无损时更集中，但传播损耗和轻微频偏都可能把这个优势吃掉。
Numbers worth keeping:
  Equal scalar input norm and initial width, z=10000d:
  peak ratio 1.589; receiver-window integrals 0.9586 vs 0.5904.
  At Gamma/omega_M=1e-4, peak ratio reverses to 0.562.
  Frequency shift +/-0.5% gives receiver-window integrals about 0.026/0.029.
Report implication:
  优先把天线、损耗和接收器放进同一个计算，比较实际收到的信号。
Source:
  research/wavepacket-prospects/demos/finite-source-caustic/results.json
  .../report.txt

F2. Detecting backflow
Reader wording:
反流不是整团波包倒着走，而是很小一部分概率短暂流回去。
原来那一点自然拖尾太细，模糊成像很容易把它淹没。
重新准备相干叠加可以保留信号，但检测次数仍然不少。
Numbers worth keeping:
  Old natural tail: sharp-window signal 3.35e-5; at blur width 0.5a the same event is not certified.
  A prepared two-carrier state has positive general witness about 0.00562 at blur 0.5a.
  With the tighter, PSF-aware classical bound, a frozen state needs about 250000 samples per ensemble,
  for three independent ensembles in the stated counting model.
Report implication:
  先问仪器能不能分辨，再优化波形；不用把更大的负流峰值直接当成更好的实验方案。
Do not compare the original late tail and new local preparation as equal-resource gain.
Source:
  research/wavepacket-prospects/demos/backflow-resolution/results.json
  .../supplemental_bound_results.json

F3. Loss compensation
Reader wording:
提前补偿损耗，能让到达的振幅和相位更接近目标，但需要付出信号强度的代价。
如果只关心包络强度，本例的收益很小。
Minimal numbers:
  Complex-field overlap F 0.799 -> 0.953.
  Absolute target weight Q 0.0771 -> 0.0459.
  Intensity-shape overlap 0.98336 -> 0.98360.
Do not present ensemble design as a demonstrated improvement:
  same-training nominal success 86.25%, ensemble 85%, only 2/160 samples apart.
Source:
  research/wavepacket-prospects/demos/loss-aware-pulse/results.json

F4. Comparing disorder
Reader wording:
起初，两类误差看起来造成很不一样的损失。
但把它们对波的反射作用调到相同后，差别几乎消失。
这次对照排除了一个看似新鲜的解释，也让后续比较更可靠。
Minimal numbers:
  Receiver probability: onsite 0.6710;
  bond with same matrix-norm budget 0.4757;
  bond with same leading backscatter strength 0.6711.
Do not use Frobenius/Born terminology in the main narrative; explain once as error budget / reflection strength.
Source:
  research/wavepacket-prospects/demos/transport-disorder/results.json

6. Matters to state once, not repeatedly

- The front half studies the single-magnon square-lattice model. The four later tests use
  separately stated simplified models borrowed from the relevant papers. One short transition
  paragraph is enough; do not burden every page with repeated model disclaimers.
- A red-blue real-amplitude picture is not a probability picture. This is central to the
  flicker story and deserves one clear side-by-side explanation.
- The original low-spreading mechanism already has direct prior work. State this plainly,
  then explain what remains useful; do not repeat "not novel" in every paragraph.
- Finite-source caustic values are normalized scalar calculations, not measured microwave gain.
  One sentence beside the result is enough.
- The loss experiment's F is phase-sensitive. Saying simply "the packet shape improved"
  would be wrong because its intensity shape barely changed.
- Whole-model evidence should be reported as "we computed / compared / verified", not
  "experimentally observed". Source experiments can be identified separately.
- code/README.md still says all Note files are historical; this blanket line is stale.
  The current single_magnon_derivation is newer and accepted. Do not propagate the stale classification.
- HTML browser interaction was blocked by environment policy, but the scientific GIFs were
  decoded and figures reviewed. Put that operational detail in the index/QA record, not the main story.
- Do not turn this report into a log of tool calls, failed preview servers, review counts,
  hashes, matrix-norm terminology, or every parameter scan. Those remain in the package index.

7. Editing guidance for the parent writer

Prefer: “接收器实际收到多少” over “有限接收窗标量权重优化”.
Prefer: “不同波长跑得是否一样快” over repeated “速度协方差／谱矩”.
Prefer: “图中颜色的变化” over “相位敏感可视化伪象”.
Prefer: “相同反射能力的误差” over unexplained “Born-matched Frobenius-budget baseline”.
Prefer: “约25万次一组，需三组” over a long list of counting estimators.
Use at most one model equation, one result table for A/B/C, and the few figures that establish the main point.
The comprehensive references, method definitions and numerical controls can remain in linked records.
